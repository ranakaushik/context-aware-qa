# Leave Policy

Applies to all full-time employees of Northwind Labs India Pvt. Ltd. Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis according to their contracted hours. The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

## Annual Leave Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave (also called earned leave or privileged leave) per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at a rate of 1.67 days per month. Employees who have completed five years of continuous service receive 25 days of annual leave per year.

## Carry Forward of Annual Leave

Employees may carry forward a maximum of 10 days of unused annual leave into the next calendar year. Any unused leave above 10 days is forfeited on 31 December. Carried-forward leave must be used by 31 March of the following year; any carried-forward days not used by 31 March are forfeited. Exceptions to the carry-forward limit require written approval from the HR Director and are granted only when leave was cancelled because of business needs.

## Casual Leave

Employees receive 8 days of casual leave per calendar year for short personal needs. Casual leave cannot be carried forward, cannot be encashed, and cannot be taken for more than 3 consecutive days.

## Requesting Leave

Annual leave requests must be submitted through the HRPortal at least 10 working days in advance for absences of three days or more. Absences of one or two days require at least 2 working days notice. Managers must approve or reject a request within 3 working days.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not carry forward and cannot be encashed. A medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days. Employees must notify their manager before 10:00 AM IST on the first day of absence.

## Parental Leave

In line with the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961, female employees receive 26 weeks of fully paid maternity leave for their first two children and 12 weeks for the third child onwards. Primary caregivers in the case of adoption or surrogacy receive 12 weeks of fully paid leave. Secondary caregivers (paternity leave) receive 4 weeks of fully paid leave, to be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption. Employees become eligible for maternity leave after 80 days of service in the 12 months before the expected date of delivery.

## Bereavement Leave

Employees receive up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, parent-in-law, or sibling) and up to 2 days for other relatives.

## Leave Encashment

Unused annual leave is not paid out during employment. On resignation or termination, up to 10 days of unused annual leave from the current year is paid out, calculated on the employee's basic salary per day. Leave encashment is subject to income tax as per the Income Tax Act, 1961. Sick leave and casual leave are never paid out.

## Public Holidays

Northwind Labs observes 12 public holidays per year. These always include the three national holidays: Republic Day (26 January), Independence Day (15 August), and Gandhi Jayanti (2 October). The remaining holidays follow the state in which the employee's office is located. The holiday calendar is published on the HRPortal every December for the following year. Employees required to work on a public holiday receive a compensatory day off, to be used within 60 days.

## Unpaid Leave

Employees with at least one year of service may request up to 30 days of unpaid leave (leave without pay) per year. Unpaid leave requires approval from both the manager and the HR Director.
