# Performance and Conduct

This document covers probation, performance reviews, promotions, and workplace conduct at Northwind Labs India.

## Probation Period

All new employees serve a probation period of 90 days. During probation, either party may end employment with 1 week of notice. After probation, the notice period is 2 months for individual contributors and 3 months for managers.

## Performance Reviews

Performance reviews take place twice a year, in September (mid-year) and March (annual, at the end of the financial year). Each review includes a self-assessment, peer feedback from at least two colleagues, and a manager assessment. Employees are rated on a 5-point scale.

## Promotions

Promotion decisions are made once a year during the March annual review cycle. An employee must have spent at least 12 months in their current level to be eligible for promotion. Promotions take effect on 1 April, the start of the financial year.

## Salary Reviews

Salaries are reviewed once a year during the annual review, and revisions take effect on 1 April.

## Performance Improvement Plans

An employee rated 1 or 2 on the 5-point scale may be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) lasting 60 days, with check-ins every two weeks.

## Workplace Conduct

Northwind Labs has zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or retaliation. Complaints of sexual harassment are handled by the Internal Committee (IC) constituted under the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 (POSH Act). Other concerns can be raised with a manager, with HR, or anonymously through the EthicsLine hotline. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is grounds for dismissal.

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any outside employment, board position, or financial interest in a competitor, supplier, or customer to HR within 30 days of it arising. Outside employment requires written approval from the employee's manager and HR.

## Gifts

Employees may accept gifts from business partners with a value of up to ₹5,000 per year from any single partner, including festival gifts such as at Diwali. Gifts above this value must be declined or reported to the Compliance team.
