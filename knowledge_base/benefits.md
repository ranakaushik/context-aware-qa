# Employee Benefits

Benefits described here apply to full-time employees of Northwind Labs India from their first day of employment unless stated otherwise. All amounts are in Indian Rupees (₹, INR).

## Health Insurance

Every employee is covered by a group mediclaim (health insurance) policy with a sum insured of ₹5,00,000 (5 lakh) per year. The policy covers the employee, spouse, up to two children, and parents or parents-in-law. The company pays the full premium for the employee, spouse, and children; the premium for parents is shared 50:50 with the employee. Employees can add or remove dependents during the annual enrollment window in March or within 30 days of a qualifying life event such as marriage or the birth of a child.

## Provident Fund and Gratuity

The company contributes 12% of the employee's basic salary to the Employees' Provident Fund (EPF), matching the employee's own 12% contribution, as required by the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952. Employees who complete 5 years of continuous service are entitled to gratuity under the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, calculated as 15 days of last drawn basic salary for each completed year of service.

## Learning and Development Budget

Each employee has a learning budget of ₹75,000 per financial year (1 April to 31 March) for courses, certifications, books, and conferences. Unused learning budget does not roll over to the next year. Purchases over ₹25,000 require manager approval before payment.

## Wellness Allowance

Employees receive a wellness allowance of ₹2,000 per month that can be used for gym memberships, yoga or fitness classes, or wellness apps. The allowance is claimed through ExpenseHub.

## Meal Card

Employees may opt for a meal card of ₹2,200 per month as part of their salary structure. The meal card can be used only for food and non-alcoholic beverages.

## Travel and Expense Benefits: Domestic Trips

Employees travelling on business within India are entitled to the following transport, stay, and per diem benefits by band. Full rules are in the Travel and Expense Policy.

| Band | Transport | Hotel stay per night (metro / Tier-2 and Tier-3 city) | Per diem per day (metro / Tier-2 and Tier-3 city) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Band A (L1–L2) | Economy air; AC 2-tier or AC chair car train; app-based cab, metro rail, or auto-rickshaw locally | ₹9,000 / ₹6,000 | ₹8,000 / ₹7,250 |
| Band B (L3–L4) | Economy air; AC 2-tier train (AC first class with manager approval); app-based or sedan cab locally | ₹9,000 / ₹6,000 | ₹9,000 / ₹8,000 |
| Band C (L5 and above) | Economy air (flexible fare); AC first class or Executive chair car train; sedan cab or self-drive rental locally | ₹12,000 / ₹9,000 | ₹10,500 / ₹9,000 |

The minimum domestic per diem is US$75 per day, paid as ₹7,250 per day (converted at the policy rate of ₹96.50 per US$1 and rounded up). Employees who use their own vehicle are reimbursed ₹12 per km for a car and ₹6 per km for a two-wheeler. Employees who stay with friends or family instead of a hotel receive ₹1,500 per night.

## Travel and Expense Benefits: International Trips

Employees travelling on business outside India are entitled to the following benefits. Country groups: Group 1 is the USA, Canada, the UK, Western Europe, Japan, Singapore, Australia, and New Zealand; Group 2 is the Middle East, Eastern Europe, Hong Kong, South Korea, Malaysia, and Thailand; Group 3 is all other countries. Full rules are in the Travel and Expense Policy.

| Country group | Hotel stay per night (Band A and B / Band C) | Per diem per day (Band A / Band B / Band C) |
|---|---|---|
| Group 1 | ₹18,000 / ₹24,000 | ₹13,500 / ₹15,000 / ₹17,000 |
| Group 2 | ₹14,000 / ₹18,000 | ₹11,000 / ₹12,500 / ₹14,000 |
| Group 3 | ₹10,000 / ₹13,000 | ₹9,650 / ₹10,500 / ₹11,500 |

The minimum international per diem is US$100 per day, paid as ₹9,650 per day (converted at the policy rate of ₹96.50 per US$1).

On international trips, day-to-day expenses — meals, local travel within the city, laundry, mobile and internet, and tips — are paid as this single per diem, loaded on the company forex card before departure, with no receipts needed. Flights, hotels, airport transfers, and travel between cities are paid separately at actual cost. Flights shorter than 6 hours are economy class for all bands. On flights of 6 hours or longer, Band B and Band C employees may fly premium economy and Band A employees fly economy; business class needs approval from the Chief Financial Officer. The company also pays for visa fees, mandatory vaccinations, and travel insurance, and provides a company forex card. Employees may take a travel advance of up to 80% of the estimated trip cost.

## Employee Assistance Program

The Employee Assistance Program (EAP) offers free and confidential counselling in English and Hindi, available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Employees and their immediate family members may use up to 6 free counselling sessions per issue per year.

## Employee Referral Bonus

Employees who refer a candidate who is hired receive a referral bonus of ₹50,000. The bonus is paid after the new hire completes their 90-day probation period. Referrals of candidates for director-level roles or above earn ₹1,00,000 (1 lakh).
