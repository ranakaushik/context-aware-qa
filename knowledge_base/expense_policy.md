# Travel and Expense Policy

This policy sets out how Northwind Labs India Pvt. Ltd. employees book business travel and claim reimbursement for business expenses. All amounts are in Indian Rupees (₹, INR) and include GST unless stated otherwise. Effective from 1 April 2026; owned by the Finance team.

## Purpose and Scope

The policy applies to all full-time employees, interns, and consultants on company assignments who travel or incur expenses on behalf of the company. Its aims are to keep employees safe and comfortable while travelling, to control costs, and to meet Indian tax and GST requirements. Travel for training or conferences paid from the learning budget follows the same rules.

## Employee Bands

Travel entitlements depend on the employee's band:

| Band | Levels | Typical roles |
|---|---|---|
| Band A | L1–L2 | Associates, engineers, analysts |
| Band B | L3–L4 | Managers, senior engineers, leads |
| Band C | L5 and above | Directors and senior leadership |

## City Categories and Country Groups

Metro cities are Mumbai, Delhi NCR (including Gurugram and Noida), Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, and Pune. All other Indian cities are Tier-2 or Tier-3 cities.

For international travel, countries are grouped by cost of living:

| Country group | Countries |
|---|---|
| Group 1 | USA, Canada, United Kingdom, Western Europe (including Germany, France, the Netherlands, and Switzerland), Japan, Singapore, Australia, and New Zealand |
| Group 2 | UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar and the rest of the Middle East, Eastern Europe, Hong Kong, South Korea, Malaysia, and Thailand |
| Group 3 | All other countries, including Sri Lanka, Nepal, Bangladesh, Vietnam, and countries in Africa |

## Travel Approval

All business travel must be approved before booking. Domestic travel is approved by the employee's direct manager. International travel requires approval from the department head and must be requested at least 21 days before departure. Travel booked without prior approval may not be reimbursed.

## Booking Travel

All flights, trains, and hotels must be booked through the company travel portal, TravelDesk, at least 14 days in advance whenever possible. Employees should choose the lowest logical fare: the cheapest option that fits the business schedule, with no more than one stop on domestic routes. Personal loyalty points may be earned, but airline or hotel choice must not be based on loyalty programmes.

## Domestic Travel: Transport

All employees travel economy class on domestic flights. Rail travel is preferred over flying for journeys of up to 6 hours, for example Mumbai–Pune or Delhi–Jaipur. Seat selection and one checked bag are reimbursed; other add-ons such as lounge access are not.

| Band | Air class | Train class | Intercity road travel | Local transport at destination |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Band A | Economy | AC 2-tier or AC chair car | AC bus or shared cab | App-based cab (Ola or Uber), metro rail, or auto-rickshaw |
| Band B | Economy | AC 2-tier, or AC first class with manager approval | Sedan cab | App-based cab or sedan cab |
| Band C | Economy (flexible fare allowed) | AC first class or Executive chair car | Sedan cab | Sedan cab or self-drive rental car |

Airport and railway station transfers are reimbursed at actual cost for all bands.

## Domestic Travel: Stay

Hotel bookings are limited to ₹6,000 per night in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities and ₹9,000 per night in metro cities for Band A and Band B employees. Band C employees may book up to ₹9,000 per night in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities and ₹12,000 per night in metro cities. The limits are per room per night, including taxes and breakfast.

| Band | Tier-2 / Tier-3 city | Metro city |
|---|---|---|
| Band A | ₹6,000 | ₹9,000 |
| Band B | ₹6,000 | ₹9,000 |
| Band C | ₹9,000 | ₹12,000 |

Hotel invoices must be GST invoices raised in the name of Northwind Labs India Pvt. Ltd. with the company's GSTIN, so that the company can claim input tax credit. For stays longer than 14 nights in one city, a company-approved serviced apartment must be booked through TravelDesk. Employees who stay with friends or family instead of a hotel receive ₹1,500 per night.

## Domestic Travel: Per Diem

During domestic business travel, employees receive a daily per diem (meal and incidental allowance) based on their band and the city they travel to:

| Band | Tier-2 / Tier-3 city | Metro city |
|---|---|---|
| Band A | ₹7,250 per day | ₹8,000 per day |
| Band B | ₹8,000 per day | ₹9,000 per day |
| Band C | ₹9,000 per day | ₹10,500 per day |

The minimum domestic per diem is ₹7,250 per day (US$75); see Minimum Per Diem and Exchange Rate.

## International Travel: Transport

Employees must book economy class for flights shorter than 6 hours. For international flights of 6 hours or longer, Band B and Band C employees may book premium economy; Band A employees travel economy. Business class requires approval from the Chief Financial Officer. Local travel within the destination city (public transport, taxis, and app-based cabs) is covered by the international per diem and is not claimed separately. Airport transfers and travel between cities abroad are reimbursed at actual cost with receipts. Car rental requires prior approval from the department head.

## International Travel: Stay

Hotel limits abroad depend on the country group and the employee's band. The limits are the rupee equivalent per room per night, including taxes and breakfast.

| Country group | Band A and Band B | Band C |
|---|---|---|
| Group 1 | ₹18,000 per night | ₹24,000 per night |
| Group 2 | ₹14,000 per night | ₹18,000 per night |
| Group 3 | ₹10,000 per night | ₹13,000 per night |

Hotels must be booked through TravelDesk. If no hotel is available within the limit near the business location, the department head may approve up to 20% more.

## International Travel: Per Diem

On international business trips, the employee's day-to-day expenses are paid as a single consolidated per diem instead of being claimed item by item. The international per diem covers all of the following, and no receipts are needed:

- meals and non-alcoholic beverages;
- local travel within the destination city (public transport, taxis, and app-based cabs);
- laundry;
- mobile roaming, local SIM cards, and internet;
- tips and small incidental expenses.

The daily rate depends on the employee's band and the country group:

| Country group | Band A | Band B | Band C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Group 1 | ₹13,500 per day | ₹15,000 per day | ₹17,000 per day |
| Group 2 | ₹11,000 per day | ₹12,500 per day | ₹14,000 per day |
| Group 3 | ₹9,650 per day | ₹10,500 per day | ₹11,500 per day |

The minimum international per diem is ₹9,650 per day (US$100); see Minimum Per Diem and Exchange Rate.

The following international trip expenses are not part of the per diem and are paid or reimbursed separately at actual cost: flights, hotel stay within the limits above, airport transfers, travel between cities abroad, visa fees, vaccinations, travel insurance, business-related printing or courier, and approved client entertainment.

## International Per Diem Payment

The full per diem for the approved number of days is loaded on the company forex card in the local currency before departure, at the rupee rates above converted at the card provider's rate on the loading date. Employees do not need to account for how the per diem is spent. If the trip is shortened, the per diem for the unused days is recovered; if it is extended with approval, the extra days are loaded on the card or paid with the expense claim. Per diem is paid for each calendar day spent abroad, including days spent travelling, and half-day rules in the Per Diem Rules section apply.

## Minimum Per Diem and Exchange Rate

The per diem has a minimum set in US dollars: US$100 per day for international business trips and US$75 per day for domestic (national) business trips within India. For payment, the minimums are converted to Indian Rupees at the company's fixed policy exchange rate of ₹96.50 per US$1, set by the Finance team and reviewed every quarter:

| Trip type | Minimum in US dollars | Conversion | Minimum per diem in rupees |
|---|---|---|---|
| International trip | US$100 per day | 100 × ₹96.50 = ₹9,650 | ₹9,650 per day |
| Domestic (national) trip | US$75 per day | 75 × ₹96.50 = ₹7,237.50, rounded up to the next ₹50 | ₹7,250 per day |

No band, city, or country group receives less than these minimums; the lowest rates in the per diem tables are set at the minimum. When Finance changes the policy exchange rate, the minimums are recalculated and any table rate that falls below the new minimum is raised to it.

## Per Diem Rules

The per diem applies to both domestic and international business travel. The domestic per diem covers meals, tips, and small incidental expenses; the international per diem also covers local travel, laundry, and communication. No receipts are needed. On the day of departure and the day of return, half the per diem is paid. Day trips of less than 8 hours receive half the per diem; trips within the employee's home city receive no per diem. If meals are provided by the host, a client, or included in a conference fee or hotel package, the per diem for that day is reduced by 50%. Alcohol is not reimbursable except at approved client entertainment events.

## Local Conveyance

Employees who use their own vehicle for business travel are reimbursed ₹12 per km for a car and ₹6 per km for a two-wheeler. Distances must be recorded in ExpenseHub with the start and end locations. App-based cabs (for example Ola or Uber), metro rail, and auto-rickshaws are reimbursed at actual cost with a receipt. Daily travel between home and the employee's regular office is not business travel and is not reimbursed.

## Travel Advance

Employees may request a travel advance of up to 80% of the estimated trip cost through ExpenseHub at least 5 working days before travel. The advance must be settled by submitting the expense claim within 15 days of returning. Unsettled advances are recovered from the next month's salary.

## International Travel: Visa, Insurance, and Forex

The company pays for visa fees, mandatory vaccinations, and travel insurance for international business trips. Foreign currency is provided on a company forex card loaded through the Finance team; employees should not exchange cash at airports. Unused foreign currency must be returned to Finance within 7 days of returning to India. Expenses abroad are reimbursed in INR at the exchange rate on the forex card statement.

## Cancellations and Changes

Cancellation and change fees are reimbursed when the change is caused by business needs and is approved by the manager. Fees caused by personal reasons are paid by the employee. Unused tickets must be cancelled through TravelDesk as soon as plans change so that refunds can be claimed.

## Combining Business and Personal Travel

Employees may add personal days to a business trip if the airfare is not higher than it would have been for the business trip alone. Hotel, per diem, and other costs for the personal days are paid by the employee.

## Client Entertainment

Client entertainment expenses are capped at ₹5,000 per person per event and must list the names and companies of all attendees. Entertainment above the cap requires prior approval from the department head.

## Submitting Expense Claims

Expense claims must be submitted through the ExpenseHub system within 30 days of the expense date. Claims submitted after 30 days require approval from the Finance Director and may be rejected. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll after approval.

## Receipts

An itemized receipt or GST invoice is required for every expense of ₹500 or more. For expenses under ₹500, a written description in ExpenseHub is sufficient. Lost receipts must be replaced by a signed Missing Receipt Declaration, which can be used at most twice per calendar year.

## Approval Limits

Expenses up to ₹50,000 are approved by the employee's direct manager. Expenses between ₹50,000 and ₹2,00,000 (2 lakh) require approval from the department head. Expenses above ₹2,00,000 require approval from the Chief Financial Officer.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following expenses are not reimbursed: traffic challans and parking fines, personal entertainment, minibar charges, laundry for trips shorter than 5 nights, upgrades that were not pre-approved, airline lounge access, expenses for family members travelling with the employee, and itemized claims abroad for meals, local travel, laundry, or communication, because these are covered by the international per diem.

## Policy Violations

Claims that break this policy are rejected or reduced to the allowed limit. Submitting false or inflated claims is misconduct and may lead to disciplinary action, including termination of employment.
