# Remote Work Policy

This policy describes how Northwind Labs India employees may work outside the office.

## Hybrid Work Model

Northwind Labs follows a hybrid model. Employees are expected to work from the office at least 3 days per week. Tuesday and Thursday are team anchor days on which every team member should be in the office. The remaining office day is chosen by the team.

## Core Hours

All employees, whether at home or in the office, must be available during core hours of 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM Indian Standard Time (IST). Meetings should be scheduled within core hours whenever possible.

## Fully Remote Arrangements

Fully remote work is available only with approval from a Vice President. Requests are reviewed every 12 months. New employees are not eligible for fully remote arrangements during their 90-day probation period.

## Home Office Equipment

Each employee receives a one-time home office stipend of ₹25,000 to buy a desk, chair, monitor, or other ergonomic equipment. GST invoices must be submitted through the expense system within 60 days of purchase. The company-issued laptop remains company property and must be returned when employment ends.

## Internet Allowance

Employees who work from home at least 2 days per week receive an internet allowance of ₹1,500 per month, paid with their monthly salary.

## Working From Another Country

Employees may work from a country outside India for up to 30 calendar days per year. Working abroad requires approval from the manager and the Legal team at least 15 working days before travel, because of tax and immigration rules.

## Working From Another City in India

Employees may work from another city within India for up to 60 calendar days per year with their manager's approval, provided they can attend their office within 48 hours if required.

## Security While Working Remotely

Employees working outside the office must connect to company systems through the corporate VPN. Working on confidential material in public places such as cafes, trains, or airports requires a privacy screen.
