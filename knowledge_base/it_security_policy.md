# IT Security Policy

All employees and contractors of Northwind Labs India must follow this policy. It supports compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passwords must not be reused across company systems and must be stored in the company password manager, KeyVault. Passwords are not required to expire on a schedule, but they must be changed immediately if a compromise is suspected.

## Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the VPN, the HRPortal, ExpenseHub, and all production systems. Hardware security keys are required for engineers with production access. SMS codes are not an accepted MFA method.

## Lost or Stolen Devices

A lost or stolen laptop or phone that has access to company data must be reported to the IT Service Desk within 1 hour of discovering the loss. A police complaint (FIR) must also be filed within 24 hours, and a copy uploaded to the Service Desk ticket. IT will remotely lock and wipe the device. The Service Desk can be reached at extension 4357 or through the #it-help channel.

## Personal Devices

Personal phones may be used for company email and chat only after they are enrolled in the company mobile device management (MDM) system. Personal laptops may not be used to access company systems.

## Software Installation

Employees may install software only from the company Software Center. Any other software requires approval from the IT Security team through a ticket in the IT Service Desk.

## Removable Media

USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops. Files must be shared using the approved company cloud storage.

## Phishing and Incident Reporting

Suspicious emails must be reported using the "Report Phishing" button in the email client. Any suspected security incident must be reported to security@northwindlabs.example within 24 hours.

## Security Training

All employees must complete security awareness training within 30 days of joining and then once every year.
